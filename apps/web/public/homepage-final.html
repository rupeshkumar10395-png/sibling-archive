The little ritual

Make it together.
Don't make it feel like a form.

The archive is designed like something you found in a drawer — personal, imperfect and full of things worth keeping.

01

You start.

Add a few memories, photos, stories, places and songs. Your sibling can't see your answers yet.

02

Send them the link.

"come on, you have to do this."

One private invite. No public profile. No social feed.

03

They add their side.

They remember the same years differently. They add their photos. They correct your version of history.

04

Then it opens.

"oh. you remembered THAT?"

Both sides become one archive — with the differences left in.
